# GoldEvidence EA v3.1 — Corrected spec (2026-11-03)

Owner instruction: fix the gaps in the proposed v3.1 plan and proceed.

## 1. Strategy = Setup B, unchanged (no M5/EMA/RSI grafting)
The proposed plan mixed v2 S1/S2 rules (M5 entry, M15/H1 EMA, RSI, retest) into "Setup B". Those rules tested negative (-0.05R to -0.17R). v3 trades Setup B exactly as validated in Python:
- Timeframe: H1 signals, D1 trend filter. Decisions on the close of a completed H1 bar; order at next bar open.
- Long: H1 close > highest high of previous 48 H1 bars AND previous D1 close > D1 SMA200.
- Short: H1 close < lowest low of previous 48 H1 bars AND previous D1 close < D1 SMA200.
- Stop: entry -/+ 2 x ATR(14) H1.
- Exit: H1 close crosses the opposite 24-bar channel (long: close < lowest low of previous 24; short: close > highest high of previous 24), OR 5 days (120 h) in trade, OR SL.
- First job of the EA: reproduce the Python trade list in the MT5 tester (trade count within 10%, mean R within 0.05R). If it does not reproduce, nothing else proceeds.

## 2. Take profit
B earns from fat-tail winners, so a fixed 2R TP would cut the edge. Every order still carries a TP (project rule), placed at 8R as a safety net; real exits come from the channel and the time stop. The "minimum 1:2" rule is satisfied because the TP is never below 2R.

## 3. AI role
Claude does not propose trades and cannot change orders in v3. It may write an after-the-fact commentary into the journal. Backtests run with no API dependency. (Reverses the v2 decision that Claude may propose trades.)

## 5. Lot feasibility (checked 2026-11-03 with live XAUUSD H1 data, price about 3,940)
- H1 ATR(14) currently about $12-18, so 2xATR stop is about $25-36 (wider on volatile days).
- Exness Standard, 0.01 lot = 1 oz: a $30 stop = $30 loss = 30% of a $100 account. IMPOSSIBLE.
- Exness Standard Cent, 0.01 cent lot is about 0.01 oz: a $30 stop = 30 USC = 0.3% of 10,000 USC. Feasible at a 0.5% budget whenever the stop is under about $50; 0.25% is not feasible.
- Conclusion: a $100 live test only works on the Cent account. Confirm the XAUUSDc contract size in MT5 (Symbol > Specification) before any live step; the EA prints the min-lot risk at startup and skips any trade above budget.

## 6. Filters (each is a new parameter, so they are limited and pre-registered)
- Spread: skip entry if spread > 10% of stop distance or > InpMaxSpreadPts.
- Regime filter: at most ONE, pre-registered: Kaufman efficiency ratio of D1 closes over 20 days; no entry if ER < 0.15. Tested only by walk-forward on 2012-2022 (dev+val). Adopt only if it improves mean R in at least 4 of 6 folds and removes no more than 40% of trades. Otherwise drop it.
- News: B was validated without a news filter. Entry-only news blackout stays OFF by default and is compared in the tester as a variant, not assumed.

## 8. Pass/fail
- MT5 reproduces Python (trade count +/-10%, mean R +/-0.05R).
- After base costs: PF >= 1.20 and mean R > 0. At 2x spread: mean R still > 0.
- Results positive in at least 2 of 3 sub-periods (2012-16, 2017-21, 2022-25).
- Monte Carlo 95th-percentile DD <= 25% at the chosen risk.
- Demo: fills, slippage and swap within 1.5x of the model; zero rule violations.
- Any failure = no live trading.
